# Front page should explain, step by step, how to get your own disc onto the PC

Order: 7000
From: mod-ideas `games/the-darkness.md` (<https://github.com/TefMeister/mod-ideas/blob/main/games/the-darkness.md>), copied 2026-09-23

`[raw]` · `[not judged]` — ⚠️ **nothing below has been checked against real hardware, a real disc
or the recompilation toolchain**

> "have on the fron page instructions on how to actually dump the disc into pc, the right dvd
> devices needed for it, the cables and motherboard needed for it, if it needs a controller on the
> back to get it physically working and all the steps to actually install it, so it runs on the pc.
> also add whatever info you have found that can help someone get this running."

The ask is a complete, hand-holding getting-started page: which drive, which cables, what plugs into
what, and every step from "I own the disc" to "the game is running on my PC" — written for someone
who has never done it.

**Why it matters more here than for our other games.** Every other project on the account starts
from a game already installed on a PC. This one cannot: there is no PC version, so *getting the game
onto the PC at all* is step zero, and it is a hardware job before it is a software one. A mod page
that skips that step is useless to almost everyone who finds it.

**The shape of what such a page would have to cover — all of it unverified:**
- **Which console version.** The project is built on the **Xbox 360** recompilation, so that is
  the disc the page has to be about. The PS3 route is a different job entirely and should not be
  mixed into the same page.
- **The drive.** Console game discs are not readable by an ordinary PC DVD drive in the normal way,
  which is the whole reason the question is being asked. Which specific drive models can do it, and
  whether they need their firmware changed, is the central unknown.
- **How it connects.** Power and data cables, and whether an ordinary desktop motherboard port is
  enough or something extra is needed in between. This is the "controller on the back" part of the
  idea — it needs a straight answer, not a maybe.
- **What the dump is then FOR.** Not an emulator — the recompilation turns the 360 build into a
  native PC program, and it needs the player's own game files as its input. The page has to say
  which files it wants and where they go, and that step is ours to document because it is ours.
- **The legal line, stated on the page itself:** own the disc, dump your own copy, nothing
  redistributed, no links to game files from anywhere. Same standard as every other project here.

**What it'd take:** an honest research pass on the drive question first, then one person actually
doing it end to end and writing down what really happened — not a page assembled from what the
internet claims. Until someone has done it once, every hardware step above is a guess. The last
step, what to do with the files once they are on the PC, is the half we will know first, because it
is our own tooling.

⚠️ **Feasibility not checked:** no drive has been bought and no disc has been dumped here. The
game already runs far enough for stereo work, so the files clearly got onto a PC somehow — the next
session on this game should simply write down how that was actually done, which is most of the page
already.
